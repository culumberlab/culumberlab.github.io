Join the lab

Research opportunities

We welcome inquiries from motivated students interested in ecology, evolution, animal behavior, physiology, genetics, and the biology of freshwater fishes.

Graduate students

MSc research

Prospective graduate students should describe their research interests, relevant experience, and the kinds of questions they hope to pursue. A CV or résumé and an unofficial transcript are helpful when making an initial inquiry.

Undergraduate students

Research experience

Undergraduates may become involved through fieldwork, laboratory experiments, animal care, data collection, and independent research projects. Opportunities depend on current projects and available space.

Interested in joining? Send a brief introduction describing your interests and experience to zwc0001@uah.edu.
